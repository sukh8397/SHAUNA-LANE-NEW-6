STAGE & COMEDY “

“I’ve had the pleasure of working with Shauna Lane on an array of Hard Headed Comedy™ productions over the years, and she brings something truly special to every stage. Her comedic instincts, versatility, and ability to fully embody a character, particularly her unforgettable Charlie, are a testament to her talent as both an actress and comedian. Shauna is engaging, fearless, and knows exactly how to connect with an audience. A consummate professional and an absolute joy to work with!”

Luz Michelle Comedian & Founder, Hard Headed Comedy™
FILM & SCREEN “

“Working with Shauna Lane was an incredible experience. Her role in the movie required filming several months after production began and she showed up to set locked in and ready to go, despite the delays. She was professional and communicative and very down to earth. It was a pleasure chatting with her and that opens up the opportunity to play with the character because you're able to have those conversations. She knows how to 'perform' and her performance added a great deal to the movie.”

Vilan Trub Director & Producer, Trub Film Co.
THEATRE & PRODUCTION “

“Shauna is a dream to work with! I cast her in my show SPARK, and we've worked on multiple productions over the course of two years (and counting!). She is talented, funny, professional and has a heart of gold. All of it shines through her work, and keeps audiences enthralled!”

Tambra McCarthy Actor, Creator & Producer of SPARK: the experience
ACADEMIC & CONFIDENCE “

“Shauna is a wonderful tutor who was able to help my 17 year-old daughter across a variety of subjects. She has the kind of personality that made it easy for her to form a connection and her sense of humor provided a necessary lightness to what sometimes can be a slog for my daughter. Prior to working with Shauna my daughter lacked confidence and interest in school, but through their work together over the course of the academic year she became much more confident and developed a more secure belief in her abilities.”

CHRIS F. Parent of 17-Year-Old Student
READING & ESL “

“Shauna is an excellent tutor for reading and ESL. She always comes prepared with age and level-appropriate materials designed specifically for the kids. My daughter loves her and is always looking forward to the lessons.”

JIM G. Parent
READING ENRICHMENT “

“My experience with Shauna has been superb. I needed help providing reading enrichment to my children at our local library. Prior to meeting with her, we spoke on the phone and she immediately understood my needs. When working with my children, she always arrives on time to our library sessions and even brings materials for the kids to work on that supports their reading and reinforces their learning. In addition, she’s great when working with them independently or as a group. She understands their distinct personalities and needs and makes necessary adjustments in the routine to keep their interest in the reading material. Shauna has truly exceeded all of my expectations. My children and I are very fortunate to work with her.”

IVETTE F. Library Reading Enrichment Parent
ESL ACCELERATION “

AMANDA R. Parent of ESL Student
