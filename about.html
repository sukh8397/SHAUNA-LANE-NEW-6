ABOUT SHAUNA

It’s always
about people.

Whether on screen, on stage or in a classroom, Shauna’s work is grounded in an interest in people and how they communicate. Her experience across performance and education brings together creativity, empathy, curiosity and storytelling.

EDUCATION & PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATIONS

A background shaped by the arts.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Studies: Fine Arts (Theater) from Central Connecticut State University, where I completed a teacher preparation program, including student teaching in a first-grade classroom.

I am a nationally certified tutor and member of the National Tutoring Association. My professional training includes Orton-Gillingham-based literacy instruction, Executive Functioning skills development, and continuing education in reading and literacy.

I have also designed original curricula and taught enrichment courses at Challenge Camp, held at Iona University in New Rochelle, NY.

My background in education, language instruction, curriculum development and the performing arts allows me to combine strong academic foundations with creativity, insight and individualized instruction. I value an approach that recognizes each learner’s strengths while making room for curiosity, confidence and growth.
